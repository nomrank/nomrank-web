## Platform

## Users
Young people in Indian cities (students and early-career, many who moved from elsewhere in India) deciding what to eat, mostly on their phones, often while standing outside or scrolling in bed. *(inferred: age and situation)*

## Product Purpose
Nomrank rates **individual dishes**, not restaurants. People give a dish 1–10 with a short hot take, argue about it (Agree / Disagree reactions), and find what's actually worth eating. Success: someone opens it, finds the best version of the dish they're craving near them, and comes back to rate it.

## Positioning
**Tastes like home**: for a dish with a home region, Nomrank shows how people *from* that region rate it ("8.7 from 12 Hyderabadis"). No restaurant-rating app can say whether the biryani passes the Hyderabadi test.

## Operating Context
Nomrank is meant for all of India; it launches in four cities: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi and Hyderabad (owner, 2026-10-29). People browse one city at a time or all of them. UI copy must not brand the product as a Bengaluru app (owner, 2026-10-21). Mobile-first PWA in the browser; native apps later. Each user picks one home region at onboarding. Spaces are leaderboards per food (Biryani, Dosa...). Ranking uses a Bayesian average.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- v1: email sign-in (magic link), username + home region onboarding, restaurants, dishes, Spaces, reviews with photos, Agree/Disagree feed, Tastes like home, reporting.
- One rating per user per dish; new accounts weigh less.
- Undecided: logo and brand mark, any photography or illustration assets.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Nomrank.
- Voice: playful, Gen-Z, celebrating home food. Never frame regions against each other.
- Terms: "Spaces", "Agree" / "Disagree" (owner, 2026-10-22: professional wording, no slang), "Tastes like home".

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles
1. The dish is the unit, never the restaurant.
2. Home is a credential, not a rivalry.
3. Opinions should be fast to give and fun to read.
4. Trust the ranking: hard to game, easy to understand.
